I love college radio, plain and simple! In addition to hosting a show every semester I've been at Northeastern, I've also served as WRBB's technical director for the past two and a half years. 

In the summer of 2023, the station completed a massive renovation and move into a new space in the Curry Student Center. I was responsible for designing, building, and installing the new studio's technical infrastructure. This included the audio routing system, the broadcast automation system, and the studio's computer network.

Additionally, during my time as technical director, we executed a full [website redesign](https://www.wrbbradio.org). I've developed and documented countless custom solutions to problems that we encounter as a college station, and I've loved every minute of it.
