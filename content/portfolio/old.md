## Grooks

[The Grooks](/old/grooks.html) are a collection of my favorite short poems by the author Piet Hein. I posted one of these each day for my friends, and archived the content in case discord stops storing old messages one day.

## DCI

I attended the Alexander Dawson School for High School. While I was there, I was the first student to complete the Distinction in Innovation program and associated award. This process involved building a number of projects, both for classes and for fun. I created a portfolio in the form of a [github wiki](https://github.com/jlefkoff/DCI-Jonah/wiki), and you can view all of the pieces there.
