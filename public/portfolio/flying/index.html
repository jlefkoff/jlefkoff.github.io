Flight

2024-02-02

As long as I can remember, I’ve been fascinated by airplanes and the world of aviation. During the pandemic, I decided to finally take the leap and pursue my private pilot’s license, and I couldn’t have been happier with the result. Learning to operate an aircraft all by myself was unforgettable, and the adventures I’ve been able to go on as a result are incredible. I got my private pilot’s license in 2021 and my instrument rating in the summer of 2023.

Flying over Monument Valley on my way back from a day trip to the Grand Canyon.

Over the Napa, CA area during my instrument training.

Flying over the Charles River in Boston, MA in a Piper Warrior.
